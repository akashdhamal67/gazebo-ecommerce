Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Gazebo's flagship physical store located?

Our heritage retail store has been situated on Mahatma Gandhi Road (M.G. Road), Camp, Pune, Maharashtra since 1979. We are open every day from 10:30 AM to 9:30 PM.

What clothing collections does Gazebo offer?

Gazebo is a dedicated family clothing destination featuring three core departments: Men's apparel, Women's & Girls' fashion, and Kids' wear, alongside curated everyday accessories.

What are the shipping charges and delivery timelines?

We offer Free Standard Delivery across India for all eligible orders. Orders dispatch directly from our Pune flagship hub within 24 to 48 business hours.

What is your exchange and return policy?

We provide a 7-day exchange window from date of delivery. You can exchange unworn items with original tags either in-person at our M.G. Road store or via scheduled courier reverse pickup.

How do I select the right size?

Each product page includes our interactive Size & Fit Guide detailing body measurements in inches for Chest, Waist, and Garment Length.

Still have questions?

Our Pune flagship team is available daily to assist you.
