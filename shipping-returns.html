Shipping & Returns Policy

1. Shipping & Pan-India Dispatch

All Gazebo orders dispatch directly from our flagship retail center on M.G. Road, Camp, Pune. We partner with premier national courier carriers to ensure swift, reliable doorstep transit across India.

  • Standard Pan-India Shipping: FREE for all qualifying orders.
  • Dispatch Time: Orders are processed and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours (excluding Sundays & public holidays).
  • Estimated Transit Time: Metro cities: 2 to 4 business days. Rest of India: 3 to 6 business days.

2. Flagship Store In-Person Pickups (Pune)

Local Pune patrons can choose same-day in-store collection at our M.G. Road store. Simply present your digital order confirmation at the counter.

3. 7-Day Hassle-Free Exchange Policy

We take pride in every garment that bears the Gazebo name. If a piece does not fit as expected or requires a size adjustment, you may request an exchange within 7 days of receiving the package.

  • Items must remain unused, unwashed, and with all original Gazebo price tags intact.
  • Exchanges can be facilitated either in-person at our M.G. Road store or via scheduled reverse courier pickup from your doorstep.

4. Support Contact

For assistance with an active dispatch or exchange request, please email contact@gazebo-ecommerce.vercel.app or speak with our store desk at +91 20 2613 0000.
